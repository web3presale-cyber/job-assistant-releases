# Job Assistant – privacy policy

Job Assistant is an internal tool used by a small team (Windows app, Android app and a Telegram bot). It is not sold
and has no server of its own.

## What "Sign in with Google" is used for

When a team member signs in with Google, Job Assistant asks for Gmail access (`https://mail.google.com/`) so the app on
that person's own computer or phone can:

- read Upwork's job alert emails and the Job Assistant messages and drafts in that person's own mailbox
  (subjects starting with "[Job Assistant]"), and
- save its own settings and search results as drafts in that same mailbox.

The app does not read, send or delete any other email, and it never sends email on its own.

## Where the data goes

- The Google sign-in (refresh token) is stored only on the person's own device, in the app's data folder.
- Email content is processed only on that device. It is not sent to the developers, to any server run by the team,
  or to anyone else.
- Job Assistant does not sell, share or use Google user data for advertising, and it does not use it to train
  any AI model.

Job Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Removing access

Sign out in the app (Settings → Gmail), or remove "Job Assistant" at
[myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Deleting the app's data folder removes
everything it stored on the device.
